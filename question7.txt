Question Title: The Missing Project Credentials

Question Link:
https://my.newtonschool.co/playground/code/7x83ezx6nimm

Solution:

grep -r "DB_PASSWORD" .
pwd
cat EventApp/config/.env > EventApp/db_password.txt

Approach:

1. Use grep -r "DB_PASSWORD" . to search recursively for the text DB_PASSWORD in files and directories under the current directory.
2. Use pwd to display the current working directory.
3. Use cat EventApp/config/.env > EventApp/db_password.txt to copy the contents of the .env file
   into db_password.txt, creating or overwriting the destination file.
