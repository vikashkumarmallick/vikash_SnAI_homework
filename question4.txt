Question Title: Deployment Project Permissions.

Question Link:
https://my.newtonschool.co/playground/code/3wz3wv7c89fo?lecture_hash=vifkrugjcwth

Solution:

chmod 700 /box/deployment/app/*.py
chmod 770 /box/deployment/datasets
chmod 770 /box/deployment/datasets/*.csv
chmod 644 /box/deployment/docs/OVERVIEW.md
chmod 755 /box/deployment/bin/*.sh

Approach:

1. I Use chmod 700 to give the owner full permissions on all Python files in the app directory.
2. I Use chmod 770 to give the owner and group full permissions on the datasets directory.
3. I Use chmod 770 to give the owner and group full permissions on all CSV files in the datasets directory.
4. I Use chmod 644 to give the owner read/write permissions and others read-only permission on OVERVIEW.md.
5. I Use chmod 755 to give the owner full permissions and others read/execute permissions on all shell scripts in the bin directory.
